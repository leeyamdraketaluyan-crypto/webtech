What I think about HARY POTTER


Harry Potter is a hugely successful modern fantasy series with immersive world-building, strong character friendships, and a darkening coming-of-age arc that turned a generation into readers—though it has some plot conveniences, uneven later pacing, and later cultural controversy around its author.
